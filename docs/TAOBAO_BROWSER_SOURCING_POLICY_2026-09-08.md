# Taobao Browser-First Sourcing Policy — 2026-09-08

Chairman approved. Effective immediately.

## Objective
Stop relying on public web search as the primary method for Taobao item discovery. Use an authenticated Taobao browser session as the preferred sourcing channel, starting from the Chairman-designated priority supplier pool.

## Operating chain
Priority supplier pool -> authenticated Taobao store browsing -> actual Taobao item record -> sourcing record -> Finance Gate -> GM approval -> Codex Shopee Excel -> manual Seller Centre upload.

For each actual supplier item capture, when available/required: store name, Taobao item ID, item URL, supplier product images, RMB purchase price, variants/colors/sizes, supply/stock status, evidence timestamp and source status.

## A/B recovery rule
Approved cases A and B remain active and never return to the candidate pool. Exact matching may be attempted, but inability to find an exact Taobao counterpart must not freeze the sourcing line. Search the priority supplier pool for commercially suitable products following the Chairman-approved design direction and product characteristics.

## Two-cycle rule
An exact-item search receives at most two working cycles. If it still does not produce usable supplier evidence, switch that sourcing effort to a suitable alternative-source/product-direction search while preserving the original case as an open sourcing exception. Do not repeat the same unsuccessful search indefinitely.

## Finance and Excel
Official Japanese SKU verification is not an Excel eligibility requirement. Finance still requires verified purchase cost plus overseas payment/card fee, freight and actual applicable Shopee fees. Current minimum net margin is 65%. Required listing/finance inputs missing -> BLOCK. Finance PASS plus complete Shopee-required listing data -> GM may approve Excel generation.

## Evidence control
Do not fabricate Taobao item URLs, prices, stock, images or variants. Browser observations must be recorded as actual supplier evidence. Third-party market pages may support market research but do not replace actual supplier purchase cost. Excel Ready remains separate from actual Shopee listing status.
