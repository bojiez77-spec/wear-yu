# Shopee Listing Gate Update — 2026-09-08

Chairman-approved operating rule, effective immediately.

For Wear-Yu Shopee Excel preparation, an official FREAK'S STORE exact SKU or an official matching product page is not required.

Do not block an otherwise complete supplier item only because those official references are unavailable.

The working Gate is based on the actual item being sold: traceable Taobao/supplier source, product images that match that supplier item, purchase cost, colors/sizes/supply status, required Shopee listing fields, and Finance Gate of at least 65% net margin after required costs.

Wear-Yu also does not require the Shopee brand field, title, or description to state `FREAK'S STORE 正品`. Do not add unsupported official/authenticity/authorization claims.

This rule overrides older internal checks that made official exact-SKU matching a mandatory Shopee Excel condition. Exact supplier-item identification and truthful listing data remain required where needed for the Shopee template and operations.

2026-09-09 implementation clarification: authenticated Taobao LOGIN PASS and Browser-First are preferred data-quality enhancements, never mandatory sourcing-start or Excel conditions. Candidates may be created from traceable screenshots, existing supplier records or public indexes with explicit gaps. Official authenticity verification is not required; unsupported claims remain prohibited. Apply the Round01 chain and A/B two-working-cycle exception rule in AGENTS.md section 16.
